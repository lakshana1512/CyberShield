About CyberShield

CyberShield is an all-in-one digital safety platform designed to create awareness about online scams, suspicious links, UPI fraud and identity misuse. It helps users understand cyber risks and follow safe digital practices.

Important Disclaimer

CyberShield is an educational awareness prototype. It uses basic rule-based checks to identify possible warning signs. It cannot guarantee that a message, website, job offer or transaction is safe or fraudulent. Always verify information through official sources.
